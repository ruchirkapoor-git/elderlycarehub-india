Our Purpose

India is home to one of the world's fastest-growing elderly populations. By 2050, nearly 320 million Indians will be above the age of 60. Yet, most navigate retirement, health challenges, isolation, and financial uncertainty without a unified support system.

Elderly Care Hub was conceived as a single-window platform to bridge that gap — connecting senior citizens, their families, and caregivers to the full spectrum of support available to them: government welfare schemes, NGO services, assisted living options, employment, and community funding.

We believe ageing is not a burden — it is a transition that deserves respect, resources, and a robust safety net. This platform is our commitment to making that a reality, city by city, family by family.

Our Five Pillars

Support System

A comprehensive safety net — helplines, counselling, home care, and emergency response — so no elder faces a crisis alone.

Schemes & Benefits

Curated information on government pensions, health programmes, legal rights, and assistive-device schemes accessible to every senior.

Old Age Homes

A trusted directory of free and paid residential care facilities across India's major cities, with verified contact details.

Employment Opportunities

Retirement need not mean withdrawal. We connect experienced seniors above 60 to part-time, consulting, and flexible work opportunities.

Community Crowdfunding

Enabling citizens to directly fund programmes, facilities, and welfare initiatives for elderly across the country.
